\section{Conclusion}

\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{Summary and Biological Significance}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Demonstrated quantitative analysis of cellular coordination during morphogenesis
    \item Revealed temporal patterns: synchronized clustering followed by coordination decay
    \item Identified key morphogenetic features driving co-clustering behavior
    \item Bilateral coordination in dorsal intercalation vs. internalization in gut formation
    \item Computational modeling provides insights into developmental mechanisms
  \end{itemize}
  
  \vspace{1em}
  \begin{block}{Key Finding}
    Cellular co-clustering exhibits distinct temporal signatures corresponding to specific morphogenetic phases in C. elegans embryonic development.
  \end{block}
