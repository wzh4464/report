\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{Data and Methodology}
  \begin{columns}[T] % align columns at the top
    \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
      \textbf{Data and Feature Engineering}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Dataset: Single-cell developmental data with lineage and temporal information.
        \item Features: Spherical harmonics computed to describe spatial characteristics of cells over time.
        \item Objective: Identify temporally and functionally coherent clusters of cells through co-clustering.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
    \hfill
    \begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
      \textbf{Methodology}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Applied a co-clustering approach to the cell $\times$ time $\times$ feature tensor.
        \item Reduced the tensor to 2D by integrating over the feature axis to analyze cell-time interactions.
        \item Visualized clustering results as a heatmap showing presence intensity of cells over time.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

  \begin{itemize}
    \item Clusters capture both temporal dynamics and cell fate similarities.
    \item Cluster 0 is broadly distributed and corresponds to common developmental trajectories.
    \item Clusters 5 and 8 show specific associations with \textit{Unspecified} and \textit{Skin} fates, suggesting functional specialization.
    \item Early-stage clusters (e.g., rightmost in the heatmap) may represent progenitor cells.
  \end{itemize}
